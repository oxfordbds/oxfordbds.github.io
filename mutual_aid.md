<p><details><summary><a><b> The Sameer Project </b></a></summary><p>

The Sameer Project is a donations based aid initiative for Gaza, led by Palestinians in the diaspora. Inside Gaza, they collaborate with local networks to run the Refaat Al Areer, Batoul and Sawarha camps, which house families at increased risk from the genocide. The team also helps families with the costs of repeated displacement, now fundraising for those wishing to return to the North of Gaza following the fragile ceasefire.

<a href="https://chuffed.org/project/149178-sameer-project-x-mass-displacement-campaign">
<button class="button_small"><h4>Support the Sameer Project</h4></button class="button_small">

## Non-Profits run by/for Palestinians

<div class="text-with-frame-black">

<p><details><summary><a><b> The Gaza Municipality </b></a></summary><p>

The Gaza Municipality provides vital services such as water supply, waste management and sewage treatment, for over 500,000 individuals across the Gaza strip. 

<a href="https://gaza-city.ensany.com/campaign/6737">
<button class="button_small"><h4>Support the Gaza Municipality</h4></button class="button_small">
</a>

</p></details></p>

<p><details><summary><a><b> Thamra </b></a></summary><p>

Thamra is a Palestinian organisation committed to fostering food sovereignty and self-sufficiency in Gaza. By revitalizing agricultural traditions and empowering communities, they aim to restore Palestinian land and ensure access to nutritious food for all. 

<a href="https://www.thamra.org/">
<button class="button_small"><h4>Support Thamra</h4></button class="button_small">
</a>

</p></details></p>

<p><details><summary><a><b> Samir Foundation </b></a></summary><p>

A UK-registered nonprofit founded during the war by medical student Ezz Lulu in honor of his father Samir. The foundation directly supports Gaza’s medical students with education, tuition, and resources because it is led by students and doctors living the crisis themselves. 

<a href="https://samirfoundation.org/">
<button class="button_small"><h4>Support the Samir Foundation</h4></button class="button_small">
</a>

</p></details></p>

<p><details><summary><a><b> The Ghassan Abu Sittah Children’s Fund </b></a></summary><p>

A project initiated by Glasgow University rector and surgeon Dr Ghassan Abu Sittah, providing medical attention to children in Gaza and Lebanon. It currently facilitates the transportation of critically ill children from Gaza to Lebanon, where they can receive expert treatment. 

<a href="https://gascf.org/pages/about-the-ghassan-abu-sittah-childrens-fund">
<button class="button_small"><h4>Support the Ghassan Abu Sittah Children’s Fund</h4></button class="button_small">
</a>

</p></details></p>

<p><details><summary><a><b> The Healthworkers4Palestine Solidarity Fund </b></a></summary><p>

A solidarity fund established by Healthworkers4Palestine. The aim is to support the families of healthcare workers who have been martyred, and who were either the main bread winners or key financial contributors for the family unit.

<a href="https://www.healthworkers4palestine.com/donate">
<button class="button_small"><h4>Support the Healthworkers4Palestine fundraiser</h4></button class="button_small">
</a>

</p></details></p>

<p><details><summary><a><b> Elham Fund </b></a></summary><p>

Established by Palestinian-Canadian psychotherapist Hammam Farah in memory of his aunt, Elham. It is a not-for-profit which distributes the money raised directly to Palestinian families in Gaza in the form of cash, utilising Elham’s extensive networks. 

<a href="https://elhamfund.ca/">
<button class="button_small"><h4>Support the Elham Fund</h4></button class="button_small">
</a>

</p></details></p>

<p><details><summary><a><b> Taawon </b></a></summary><p>

A non-profit launched by Palestinian business leaders and intellectuals in 1983. In partnership with the Bank of Palestine Group, they recently launched Noor’s Program, aiming to support 20,000 children orphaned by the genocide. 

<a href="https://www.taawon.org/en/noor">
<button class="button_small"><h4>Support the Noor Project</h4></button class="button_small">
</a>

</p></details></p>

<p><details><summary><a><b> Medical Aid for Palestinians </b></a></summary><p>

Established by Dr Swee Ang following the deadly Sabra and Shatila massacres in 1982, MAP provides vital medical aid in emergencies, including the past two years of genocide. MAP’s local team are themselves Palestinians from Gaza, and they run projects in partnership with Palestinian organisations, doctors, nurses and other health professionals, which ensure that these are both wanted and needed by the communities they serve. 

<a href="https://www.map.org.uk/">
<button class="button_small"><h4>Support MAP</h4></button class="button_small">
</a>

</p></details></p>

<p><details><summary><a><b> Connecting Humanity </b></a></summary><p>

A non-profit organisation that connects vulnerable people in conflict zones. Has provided e-sims in Gaza since October 2023. 

<a href="https://connecting-humanity.org/">
<button class="button_small"><h4>Support Connecting Humanity</h4></button class="button_small">
</a>

</p></details></p>
